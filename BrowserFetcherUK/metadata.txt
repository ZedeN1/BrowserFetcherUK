description=Adds UK government map services (DEFRA / Environment Agency, Scotland, Wales, BGS, CEH) to the QGIS Browser as WMS, WFS, WCS, XYZ and vector tile connections, and keeps them up to date.
version=0.03
author=Pavel Diptan
email=pdiptan@gmail.com
about=Searches the public data catalogues of England (data.gov.uk, or the complete DEFRA catalogue), Scotland (Spatial Data Scotland) and Wales (Natural Resources Wales), picks out every dataset with a map service, and adds them to the QGIS Browser as connections (about 3,000), together with BGS, UKCEH, OpenStreetMap and Mapzen services.

    - Fast fetch takes under a minute; Slow fetch uses the complete DEFRA catalogue (adds Marine Management Organisation datasets missing from data.gov.uk) and takes an hour or more, resuming where it stopped if interrupted. Datasets only a slow fetch finds are kept by later fast fetches.
    - Fetching only saves a copy of the list; Apply to QGIS Browser writes it to the Browser after you have seen what changes. Only the connections the plugin added are replaced; yours are never touched.
    - A shared folder (e.g. on a network drive) lets one person fetch and everyone else apply in seconds. An update lock stops two people fetching at once.
    - Shows what changed (new, removed, updated and retired datasets, with their source) and keeps a history.
    - Choose which built-in and custom connections to add (import QGIS connection XML files or pick them from the Browser) and edit the list of catalogues searched; both can be exported and shared.
